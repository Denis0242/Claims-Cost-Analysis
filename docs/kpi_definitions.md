# KPI Definitions

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Total Claim Cost | Sum of all claim amounts |
| Average Claim Amount | Average cost per claim |
| Total Claims | Count of unique claims |
| YoY % Change | Percent change in claim cost between current and prior year |
| Region Share | Region claim cost divided by total claim cost |
| Claim Type Share | Claim type cost divided by total claim cost |
| Severity Cost | Claim cost grouped by severity level |
